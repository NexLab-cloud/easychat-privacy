Privacy Policy

Easy Chat: easy message  •  com.nexlab.easychat

Last updated: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NexLab ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile application Easy Chat: easy message ("the App"). We built the App to respect your privacy: it works without an account, and most of what it does happens entirely on your device. This policy describes what is collected, why, and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, or messages.
  • Advertising and purchase-related identifiers are collected by Google services (AdMob and Google Play Billing) to show ads and process subscriptions.
  • Features such as the Status Saver, App Lock, and Message Log operate on your device only — that data is never sent to us or any server.
  • Data transmitted off the device is encrypted in transit.

Data We (and Our Providers) Collect

We do not run our own servers that store your personal information. However, third-party services embedded in the App collect certain data as described below. This aligns with the App's Google Play Data safety disclosure.

Data typePurposeCollected / Shared
Device or other IDs (e.g. advertising ID) To display and measure advertisements Collected & shared by Google AdMob
Purchase history To process and restore subscriptions (Easy Chat Pro) Collected & shared by Google Play Billing
App activity & diagnostics Ad delivery, crash and performance reporting Collected by Google AdMob / Google Play services

"Shared" means the data is transmitted to Google acting as an independent service provider for the purposes above. We do not sell your data.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may access your device's advertising ID and related information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads. Subscribing to Easy Chat Pro removes ads within the App. AdMob's handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions & Purchases

Optional subscriptions (Easy Chat Pro, monthly or yearly) are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Google. The App only receives confirmation of whether an entitlement is active, in order to unlock features and remove ads.

On-Device Features (No Data Leaves Your Device)

Status Saver

To let you save WhatsApp statuses, the App asks you to grant access to WhatsApp's status folder through Android's system folder picker (the Storage Access Framework). The App reads only that folder, only to display and save statuses you choose. Nothing from this folder is uploaded or shared; saved files go to your device's own gallery.

App Lock

You may protect the App with a PIN or biometric (fingerprint / face) lock. Biometric verification is performed by your device's operating system; we never receive your biometric data. Your PIN is stored only on your device in hashed form.

Message Log (Notification Access)

If you enable the Message Log feature, the App uses Android's notification access to help you view messages you may have missed. This information is processed and stored on your device only It is never transmitted to us or to any external server, and you can turn notification access off at any time in your device settings. You can clear this stored data from within the App.

Preferences & History

Settings, recent numbers, favourites, saved message templates, and scheduled messages are stored locally on your device so the App remembers your choices. This stays on your device and is removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

How Data Is Handled

  • Encryption in transit: data sent off the device (to Google's ad and billing services) is transmitted over encrypted connections.
  • Retention: we do not retain personal data on our own servers. On-device data remains until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data held by Google is retained per Google's policies.
  • No sale of data: we do not sell personal information.

Your Choices & Controls

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Remove ads entirely by subscribing to Easy Chat Pro.
  • Revoke folder access, notification access, or biometric permission at any time in Android settings.
  • Clear all locally stored data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing / Google Play services — Google Privacy Policy
